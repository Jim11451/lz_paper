# 投稿准备与英文摘要备选

## 投稿顺序

建议以 **Physical Review D Regular Article** 为首选，用同一份英文主稿准备 **JHEP** 和 **EPJ C** 版本。三个版本共用参数定义、图表和数值，标题与摘要分别强调跨实验唯象、对撞机机制或分支的可复现性。期刊的取舍取决于主排除边界的验证程度和作者希望突出的结果。 [PRD 作者指南](https://journals.aps.org/prd/authors) · [JHEP 研究范围](https://link.springer.com/journal/13130/aims-and-scope) · [EPJ C 研究范围](https://link.springer.com/journal/10052/aims-and-scope)

文章的问题是，在夸克质量加权的矢量有效模型中，哪些匹配 LZ 参考反冲率和 Planck 热遗迹丰度的放热型伪狄拉克暗物质解，可以由 CMS 单喷注数据检验。轻夸克决定领先阶相干氙反冲，重夸克驱动对撞机产生。逐点求解把这两个量联系到同一组参数，并给出在壳与离壳分支。对于满足窄宽条件的在壳分支，论文再给出 CMS 观测上限。这个定位承接 Gemmell、Hooper 和 Krnjaic 的放热反冲基准，与 Du、Huang 和 Xie 的吸热型重中介子 dijet 分析互补。Cox 等已研究过偏好顶夸克的矢量中介子，本文的贡献应落在具体的反冲率匹配、丰度分支和 CMS 缺失横动量检验上。 [放热反冲基准](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26570) · [重中介子 dijet 分析](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07225) · [早期 top-philic 矢量模型](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00471)

## 投稿前的工作

1. **优先量化 monojet 边界的探测器依赖。** 先用相同事件重建标称和变动 $b$-tag 效率、误标率的分箱模板，分别检查圈图和树图。在三个强灵敏点之外，挑选观测 $\mu_{95}$ 接近 1、覆盖低质量边界的点复拟合。附录 C 的两个验证应同时保留。使用 CMS 官方模板的统计复算检验似然。用自制模板复算时，100 GeV 官方矢量基准的绝对产额只复现了 62.9%，其余差异仍需归因。在信号接受率误差得到量化后，给出边界的变化范围。
2. **给主结果保留一个扫描内部的代表点。** 目前 $(M_V,g_t)=(112\,\mathrm{GeV},2.747)$ 的名义观测上限为 $0.540$。该点适合做与三个高灵敏度基准相同的补样和接受率检验，以检验灵敏度是否也稳健地出现在耦合上界以内。正文应并列展示反冲谱、丰度分支和 CMS 排除图，并在排除图附近交代信号模板验证的精度。
3. **顶夸克伴随产生按现有证据写。** 当前可引用完整矩阵元得到的离壳 $t\bar t+\mathrm{MET}$ 真值截面。若合著者希望加入定量 $t\bar t+\mathrm{MET}$ 排除图，先用同一参数卡核对 LZ 核反冲归一化，再逐点重算丰度、使用与质量解一致的信号模板，并以独立背景约束或公开联合似然验证 observed。组员目前的审核列出了信号模板暗质量偏差、CMS 后拟合背景重复使用和效率对照差异。单顶保留为对部分子能量切口敏感的探索结果。 [组员的复核答复](RESPONSE_zh.md)
4. **整理英文 LaTeX 源稿和复现材料。** 主图附上逐点参数与分支、CMS 模板和输入、拟合脚本及可运行说明。核对作者顺序、单位、通讯作者、经费、数据可用性和文献标识符。作者审阅通过后发布 arXiv。若选择 JHEP，其作者指南要求已有 arXiv 编号，且送审稿与 arXiv 版本一致。 [arXiv 提交流程](https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit/index.html) · [JHEP 作者指南](https://jhep.sissa.it/jhep/help/JHEP/JHEP_author.jsp)

## Physical Review D

**Title**

Xenon recoils and CMS monojet constraints on exothermic pseudo-Dirac dark matter

**Abstract**

We investigate whether the CMS monojet search can test an exothermic pseudo-Dirac dark matter model motivated by the high-energy xenon recoil candidate reported by LUX-ZEPLIN. In the electroweak-broken effective model, a vector mediator couples to quarks in proportion to their masses. Light quarks set the leading coherent xenon response, while heavy-quark couplings drive collider production. At each mediator mass and top-quark coupling, we match a reference truth-level yield in the LUX-ZEPLIN recoil window and solve for the dark matter mass and coupling that reproduce the Planck thermal relic abundance. We recast 66 public missing-transverse-momentum bins from the CMS Run 2 monojet search for the on-shell solutions that satisfy the narrow-width criteria. The observed 95% confidence-level limits fall below the nominal signal strength at a subset of these solutions. Three representative points remain excluded when the recoil-yield target is varied by 25% in either direction and when independently generated, stratified signal templates are used.

## JHEP

**Title**

Loop-induced monojet production of exothermic pseudo-Dirac dark matter with a light vector mediator

**Abstract**

We study missing-transverse-momentum signatures of exothermic pseudo-Dirac dark matter coupled to quarks through a mass-weighted vector current in the electroweak-broken phase. Matching a reference xenon recoil yield and the Planck thermal relic abundance gives distinct on-shell and off-shell solutions, which can coexist at the same mediator mass and top-quark coupling. For the on-shell narrow-width solutions, we construct monojet signal templates from tree-level production and the quark-loop process $gg\to gV$. Using the public binned CMS Run 2 data, we obtain observed 95% confidence-level exclusions for part of this solution set. The loop process dominates the selected signal at three sensitive benchmarks. Independent signal samples with different generator-level strata retain the exclusion at all three points, while refitting the grid with the pooled templates shifts the contour. For lighter mediators, we use the full matrix element to calculate the production of a dark matter pair through an off-shell mediator in association with a top-quark pair.

## EPJ C

**Title**

Thermal relic branches and CMS monojet sensitivity for exothermic pseudo-Dirac dark matter

**Abstract**

We map the thermal relic solutions of an exothermic pseudo-Dirac dark matter model with a vector mediator whose quark couplings are proportional to the quark masses. For each mediator mass and top-quark coupling, we solve for the dark matter mass and coupling that reproduce a reference truth-level yield in the LUX-ZEPLIN xenon recoil window and the Planck dark matter abundance. We find that on-shell and off-shell solutions can coexist at the same coordinates in the projected parameter plane. We use 66 public missing-transverse-momentum bins from the CMS Run 2 monojet search to derive expected and observed 95% confidence-level limits for the on-shell solutions that satisfy the narrow-width criteria. The observed limits exclude a subset of the selected solutions. At three representative points, the exclusion persists when we vary the recoil-yield target by 25% and compare independently generated signal templates. Replacing the templates at three mass nodes changes the observed exclusion contour while retaining the lowest excluded mass sampled on the grid.
